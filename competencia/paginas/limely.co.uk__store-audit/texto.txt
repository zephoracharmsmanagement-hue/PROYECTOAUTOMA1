What counts as the "first fix"?

After the audit we pick the single highest-impact improvement we can build in a day or less. Usually a checkout tweak, a product page upgrade, a speed fix, or a navigation change. We design it, build it, and push it live to your store at no cost.

Is this genuinely free? What's the catch?

There is no catch. The audit is free, the call is free, the fix is free. A fair share of the stores that see the value of our thinking go on to work with us on a bigger project. That is the only ask. Plenty take the audit and the fix and that is the end of it.

What if my store does less than £10k/month?

We focus this offer on UK Shopify and Magento stores doing £10k+ per month because that is where our work creates the biggest impact.

Which platforms do you cover?

Shopify, Shopify Plus, Magento 2 and Hyvä Magento. If you are on a niche platform we can still do the audit but the first fix may not be possible without a quote.

How long does the whole thing take?

From form submission to audit call is usually within a week. The Loom walkthrough lands within 3 days of the call. The first fix is shipped within 14 days, often sooner.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, happily. Send us yours when you submit the form and we will sign it before the audit call.

Why are you doing this for free?

Because we know our work earns its keep. The audit shows you exactly how we think, the fix shows you exactly how we ship. If you like what you see, we are an obvious choice for the next project. If you don't, you have walked away with a free improvement to your store. We are happy either way.
